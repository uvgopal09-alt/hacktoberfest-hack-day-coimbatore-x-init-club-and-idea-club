## Suggested questions

- When does the library open?
- Where can I park my bike?
- How do I replace a lost ID card?
- When does the canteen serve lunch?

## Library

Keywords: opening hours, timings, closed
The library is open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Parking

Keywords: bike, bicycle, car, vehicle
Bicycles can be parked at the racks next to the main gate. Cars must use the parking area behind the admin block.

## ID card

Keywords: identity card, lost card
New students get their ID card in the first week. For a lost card, visit the college office to get a replacement.

## Canteen

Keywords: cafeteria, food, mess, breakfast, lunch, timings
The canteen serves breakfast and lunch on working days, from 8 AM to 3 PM.

## College buses

Keywords: bus, transport, shuttle, timings
Buses leave from the main gate at 7:30 AM and 4:30 PM on working days.

## Fees

Keywords: fee, payment, pay
Fees can be paid online or at the college office. Check the notice board for due dates.

## Wi-Fi

Keywords: wifi, internet
Campus Wi-Fi is available in the library and seminar halls. Log in with your student ID details.

## Exams

Keywords: exam, test, timetable
Exam timetables are posted on the notice board two weeks before exams.

## College office

Keywords: office, contact, phone, admin
The college office is open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 4 PM. Visit the office in person for any other help.
